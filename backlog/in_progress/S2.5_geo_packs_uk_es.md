# S2.5 — Geo job-portal packs: UK + Spain

**Epic:** Sprint 2 — MCP v1 (packs) / geo expansion
**Estimated effort:** ~2 h
**State:** pending

## Goal

Ship country packs for the UK (ISO `GB`) and Spain (`ES`) so a candidate in
those markets gets real, working per-source search URLs from `hunt_plan`. Each
portal's `search_playbook.url_template` must be BROWSER-VERIFIED to actually
return job listings (not a hand-guessed pattern).

## Spec

- `packs/uk.yaml` (kind: country, countries `[GB]`): the major UK boards —
  Indeed UK, Reed, Totaljobs, LinkedIn UK, CV-Library — each with a verified
  `{query}` search template. General boards `sectors: [any]`.
- `packs/es.yaml` (kind: country, countries `[ES]`): InfoJobs (the dominant ES
  portal), Indeed ES, LinkedIn ES, Tecnoempleo (tech), InfoEmpleo — verified
  templates. Spanish-language portals still take an English/whatever `{query}`.
- Country codes are ISO-3166 alpha-2: `GB` (not "UK") and `ES`. The client LLM
  normalizes user-typed "UK" → `GB` at intake.
- Verification: navigate each `search_url` (query "developer") in a real browser
  and confirm job listings render; encode the confirmed URL pattern.
